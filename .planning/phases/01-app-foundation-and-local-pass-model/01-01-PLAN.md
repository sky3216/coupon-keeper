<objective>
Create the thinnest launchable Flutter app skeleton: a Material 3 app with Today, Wallet, and Scan bottom navigation, real first-run empty states, and CTA navigation from Today/Wallet into Scan.
</objective>

<user_story>
As a first-time Coupon Keeper user, I want to open the app and move from the Today scan CTA to the Scan tab, so that I immediately understand where to start finding hidden coupons.
</user_story>

<must_haves>
- SHELL-01: App scaffold supports iOS and Android Flutter launch.
- SHELL-02: Bottom navigation has exactly `Today`, `Wallet`, and `Scan`.
- SHELL-03: First-run empty states use product copy from `01-UI-SPEC.md`, not placeholders.
- SHELL-04: Primary actions remain reachable and readable on a 320x568 logical screen.
- D-01: Today is scan CTA centered.
- D-02: Wallet is quiet result space; Scan is action space.
- D-03: Today copy is loss-prevention, not photo cleanup.
- D-04: CTA moves to Scan tab only; no picker/OCR/scan flow in Phase 1.
- UI-SPEC: Flutter Material 3, quiet wallet utility tone, exact screen copy, 44px/48px tap targets.
</must_haves>

<truths>
- D-01: Today first screen is scan CTA centered.
- D-02: Wallet is quiet result space and Scan is action space.
- D-03: Today empty-state tone is loss prevention, not photo cleanup.
- D-04: Today and Wallet scan CTAs move to the Scan tab only in Phase 1.
</truths>

<context_decision_coverage>
- D-01: Today 첫 화면은 스캔 CTA 중심으로 만든다. 첫 사용자는 앱 설명을 오래 읽기보다 숨은 쿠폰을 찾는 행동으로 바로 이어져야 한다.
- D-02: Wallet은 조용한 결과 공간, Scan은 행동 공간으로 구분한다. Wallet은 저장된 pass가 모이는 곳이고, Scan은 사용자가 guided scan을 시작하는 곳이다.
- D-03: Today의 빈 상태 문구와 화면 톤은 손실 방지형으로 잡는다. "사진 정리"보다 "잊고 있던 쿠폰을 찾아 손실을 막는다"는 가치가 먼저 보여야 한다.
- D-04: Phase 1에서 Today/Wallet의 스캔 CTA를 누르면 실제 스캔 플로우를 열지 않고 Scan 탭으로 이동한다. 실제 사진/파일 선택과 batch scan shell은 Phase 2에서 구현한다.
</context_decision_coverage>

<threat_model>
| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Phase 1 accidentally requests broad photo/download permissions | high | Do not add photo/file picker packages or platform permission prompts in this plan. CTA only changes tabs. |
| Placeholder or fake coupon data weakens trust | medium | Do not render fake coupon cards, fake protected value, fake counts, `TODO`, `Lorem ipsum`, or `Coming soon`. |
| Flutter SDK missing blocks execution | high | First task verifies `flutter --version`; if missing, stop and install/configure Flutter before scaffold. |
</threat_model>
