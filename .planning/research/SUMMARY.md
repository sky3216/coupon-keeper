# Research Summary

**Generated:** 2026-05-21
**Project:** Coupon Keeper

## Stack

Use Flutter for shared UI/application logic and native Swift/Kotlin platform adapters for photos/files, OCR, notifications, and purchases. Use SQLite and app-internal image copies for local durability.

## Table Stakes

Guided scan, OCR candidates, user confirmation, local pass wallet, pass detail, expiry reminders, manual fallback, used/expired states, and no-server privacy are required for v1.

## Differentiators

The first scan discovery report, useful free reminders, cleanup candidate flow, contextual Pro gate, and pass-centric model are the distinctive parts of the MVP.

## Watch Out For

Do not ask for broad photo access at first launch. Do not trust OCR silently. Do not make the wallet a decorative card grid. Do not hide the core reminder value behind Pro. Do not auto-delete source images.
